🌾 KisanConnect
Pakistan’s Smart Farming Platform

KisanConnect is a modern agriculture platform made for Pakistani farmers to help them grow smarter, earn better, and access everything they need in one place.
This project combines AI, live market data, weather forecasting, GPS mapping, and farmer community support into a single easy-to-use platform. 
The goal is to make farming more digital, smart, and accessible for every farmer in Pakistan.

🚜 What Makes KisanConnect Special?

Most agriculture platforms focus on only one thing like weather updates or crop prices.

KisanConnect brings everything together:

✅ AI Crop Advisor
✅ Live Mandi Rates
✅ Smart Weather Alerts
✅ Urdu Voice Support
✅ GPS Field Mapping
✅ Farmer Community
✅ Government Schemes
✅ Smart Farming Calculators

All designed specifically for Pakistani farmers 🇵🇰

✨ Main Features

🤖 AI Crop Advisor
Farmers can get AI-based suggestions about:
Crop diseases
Pest attacks
Fertilizer usage
Harvest timing
Users can even upload crop images for quick diagnosis.

📊 Live Mandi Rates

Real-time mandi prices from different cities of Pakistan.
Farmers can:
Compare market rates
Track crop prices
Find better selling opportunities

🌤 Smart Weather System
The platform provides:
7-day weather forecast
Rain alerts
Heatwave warnings
Crop-specific weather advice

This helps farmers protect crops and plan activities better.

👨‍🌾 Farmer Community

A space where farmers can:
Ask questions
Share experiences
Learn from others
Get expert advice
Supports both Urdu and English.

🔊 Urdu Voice Support

Many farmers are not comfortable reading long text.
KisanConnect includes voice support in Urdu so users can:
Speak questions
Hear responses
Use the platform more easily
🗺 GPS Field Mapping
Farmers can map their land and monitor:
Farm areas
Irrigation zones
Soil conditions
Satellite-based insights

📋 Government Schemes

The platform also helps farmers stay updated with:
Subsidies
Agriculture loans
Kisan cards
Government farming programs
